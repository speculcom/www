3D 与数字人，按顺序找资料3D and digital humans: resources in order

3D 与数字人共同卡在三件事：几何是否可用、绑定与动作是否自然、渲染与实时性是否够。先定交付形态（静帧、可交互模型、短视频、实时数字人），再选生成方式与管线。3D and digital humans share three constraints: usable geometry, natural rigging and motion, and enough rendering and real-time performance. Fix the deliverable first (still, interactive model, short video, real-time avatar), then choose the generation path and pipeline.

路线顺序不代表推荐排名；没有实测的能力对比、成本降幅与成功率，站群一律写未知。Route order is not a ranking; where no measured comparison, cost reduction or success rate exists, the sites record it as unknown.
