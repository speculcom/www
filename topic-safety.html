主题路线Topic route

安全与合规，按顺序找资料Safety and compliance: resources in order

安全不是加一句「请勿有害」：先定威胁模型，再看评测与事件记录，然后落到权限、数据与日志。合规也不是一张清单，而是把适用法规、证据与责任人分开写清。Safety is not one added sentence: define the threat model, look at evaluations and incident records, then implement permissions, data handling and logs. Compliance is not one checklist either; separate the applicable rules, the evidence and the owner.

路线顺序不代表推荐排名；没有实测的能力对比、成本降幅与成功率，站群一律写未知。Route order is not a ranking; where no measured comparison, cost reduction or success rate exists, the sites record it as unknown.

边界Boundary

本站不是法律意见，也不替任何组织做合规结论；法规原文、监管解释与合同条款优先。安全评测分数只在对应威胁模型与测试集下成立，不能拿一个基准证明「整体安全」。This is not legal advice and does not issue a compliance conclusion for any organisation; statutes, regulator interpretations and contracts govern. Safety scores hold only for their threat model and test set; one benchmark cannot prove “safe overall”.
