领域深度 · 中国区可用性Field guide · China availability

中国区可用性：能不能用、多少钱、数据放哪China availability: can you use it, what does it cost, where does data live?

“支持中国”不是一句话。你至少要填六列：能不能开通、在哪个区域、按什么计价、数据留在哪、许可证允许什么、合同和发票由谁出。每一格都要有官方入口和核验日。空白就写未知，不要用“应该没问题”补。“Supports China” is not one line. You need six columns: can it be activated, in which region, how it is priced, where data stays, what the licence allows, and who issues the contract and invoice. Every cell needs an official entry point and a check date. Write “unknown” for blanks; do not fill them with “probably fine”.

本页是采购与核对的判断框架，不构成法律、合规或价格结论。供应商可用性、价格、合同与数据条款以官方文档、合同和现场核验为准。This is a procurement and verification framework, not a legal, compliance or pricing conclusion. Vendor availability, prices, contracts and data terms remain with official documents, signed contracts and on-site verification.

拿一个具体活走一遍One concrete job

假设一个具体活：你在上海，要给 20 人团队选一个模型 API，要求能开票、数据在境内、按 token 计价。先填这六列，再约三家对比。Take one concrete job: in Shanghai, choose a model API for a 20-person team, with a Chinese invoice, domestic data residency and token pricing. Fill the six columns, then compare three vendors.

先把六列写成可核对的矩阵，再谈模型好坏。同一家厂商的“中国区”不等于同一份合同、同一片区域、同一种价格。Write the six columns into a verifiable matrix before comparing models. “China” under one vendor is not the same contract, the same region or the same price.

1 · 六列，不要合成一个“支持中国”1 · Six columns, not one “available in China”

把“支持中国”拆成六列，是为了让每个答案都能被核对。可用性回答“能不能开通、模型在不在售”。价格回答“按 token、小时、席位还是最低消费”。数据驻留回答“训练、推理、日志、备份、支持访问分别在哪”。许可证回答“模型、权重、输出、再分发分别允许什么”。采购回答“合同主体、发票、代理、验收和 SLA”。支持回答“中文工单、故障响应、版本更新”。Split “available in China” into six columns so every answer can be checked. Availability asks whether an account can be opened and the model is actually offered. Price asks token, hour, seat or minimum spend. Residency asks where training, inference, logs, backups and support access sit. Licence asks what is permitted for model, weights, output and redistribution. Procurement asks contract entity, invoice, reseller, acceptance and SLA. Support asks Chinese tickets, incident response and version updates.

六列不能互相替代。模型在售，不代表合同允许你的用途。数据留在境内，不代表日志或备份不出境。价格低，不代表包含支持或退费。有发票，不代表合同主体能承担数据责任。采购前把六列都写成“有证据 / 未知 / 不适用”，不要只写“支持”。The six columns cannot substitute for one another. A model being offered does not mean the contract permits your use. Data staying in-country does not mean logs or backups do. A low price does not include support or refunds. An invoice does not mean the contracting entity carries data responsibility. Before purchase, mark each column “evidence / unknown / not applicable”, not just “supported”.

2 · 按交付形态分行：云 API、专属、私有化、本地权重、开源、集成商2 · Rows by delivery form: cloud API, dedicated, private, local weights, open source, integrator

“同一家厂商”可能同时卖云 API、专属实例、私有化软件和硬件一体机，四者的数据路径、价格和合同完全不同。按交付形态分行，才不会把“云 API 可用”误写成“私有化也能用”。One vendor may sell cloud API, dedicated instances, private software and appliances at the same time. Their data paths, prices and contracts differ. Rows by delivery form prevent “cloud API is available” from becoming “private deployment works too”.

开源仓库又是另一种形态：代码或许可证公开，不等于权重可商用，也不等于托管服务可用。把“能下载”与“能生产”分开写。把“社区版”与“企业版”分开写。An open repository is another form: public code or a licence does not mean commercial weights or a hosted service. Separate “can download” from “can run in production”, and “community edition” from “enterprise edition”.

3 · 供应商入口不是结论3 · A vendor entry point is not a conclusion

下表只给官方入口和“先核哪三件事”，不给可用性、价格或许可结论。所有状态默认“待核”。入口能打开，只说明链接有效，不说明你的区域、用途或合同能通过。核完后把日期写进最后一列，才算有证据。The table below gives only official entry points and “first three things to check”. It does not conclude availability, price or licence. Every status starts as “to verify”; an opening link only means the link works, not that your region, use or contract passes. Write the date in the last column after checking.

供应商的“中国区”可能指中国内地区域、国际站的中国客户、或仅中文界面。三者不是一回事。采购前用合同主体、服务区域和发票抬头三个词对齐，别用“官网有中文”推断“能在国内签约”。A vendor’s “China” may mean mainland regions, Chinese customers on an international site, or only a Chinese interface. These are not the same. Align contract entity, service region and invoice issuer before purchase; do not infer “can sign in China” from “the website has Chinese”.

4 · 数据驻留：训练、推理、日志、备份、支持访问4 · Residency: training, inference, logs, backups and support access

“数据留在境内”经常只指推理请求的存储位置，不包含日志、备份、监控、工单附件和跨境支持访问。把数据流画成五条：训练数据、推理输入、输出、日志与备份、支持/运维访问。每条都问“存在哪、谁能访问、留多久、怎么删”。“Data stays in-country” often means only where inference requests are stored. It may exclude logs, backups, monitoring, ticket attachments and cross-border support access. Draw five flows: training data, inference input, output, logs and backups, support/operations access. For each ask where it sits, who can access it, how long it is kept and how it is deleted.

合同里要能落到具体区域、具体子处理者和具体保留期。只写“遵守当地法律”不够。只写“数据加密”也不够，加密不改变数据所在地和访问者。若厂商用海外支持团队，必须单独写支持访问的审批、脱敏与审计。The contract needs specific regions, subprocessors and retention periods. “Complies with local law” is not enough; “encrypted” is not enough either, because encryption does not change location or who can access it. If support is handled abroad, write separate approval, redaction and audit for support access.

5 · 价格：token、小时、席位、最低消费5 · Price: tokens, hours, seats and minimum spend

价格页上的单价不是账单。还要算最低消费、承诺用量、出网流量、存储、日志、支持、税费、代理服务费和失败重试。按“每个通过验收的任务”或“每个成片分钟”做分母，才能和自建、外包或其他厂商比较。The unit price on a price page is not the bill. Add minimum spend, committed volume, egress, storage, logs, support, tax, reseller fees and retries. Use “per accepted task” or “per finished minute” as the denominator so the number is comparable with in-house, outsourcing or another vendor.

把价格拆成三段：固定（最低消费/平台费/支持）、用量（token/小时/席位/存储）、变动（出网/重试/人工复核）。只比用量单价，常常会漏掉固定和变动。只比首月优惠，常常会漏掉续费与退出成本。Split price into three parts: fixed (minimum/platform/support), usage (tokens/hours/seats/storage), and variable (egress/retries/human review). Comparing only the usage unit often misses fixed and variable costs; comparing only the first-month discount often misses renewal and exit costs.

为什么：换供应商的真实成本Renewal/exit

6 · 许可证与合同：模型、权重、输出、再分发6 · Licence and contract: model, weights, output and redistribution

模型服务条款、权重许可证、输出使用条款、再分发条款是四份不同的东西。云 API 通常只给你“按服务使用”的权利，不给你权重。本地权重可能允许商用但限制再分发。输出条款可能允许你使用生成内容，但不允许你声称模型是你自己的。Model service terms, weights licence, output terms and redistribution terms are four different documents. A cloud API usually grants service use, not weights. Local weights may allow commercial use but restrict redistribution. Output terms may allow you to use generated content but not to claim the model is yours.

采购合同要把“谁能用、用于什么、多少量、多久、能否再分发、终止后怎么办”写进去。开源许可证不能替代合同。合同也不能替代许可证。两者冲突时，先停下来让法务与供应商书面澄清。The procurement contract should state who may use it, for what, at what volume, for how long, whether redistribution is allowed and what happens after termination. An open-source licence does not replace a contract, and a contract does not replace a licence. If they conflict, stop and get written clarification from legal and the vendor.

7 · 证据等级与核验日7 · Evidence levels and check dates

同一句“支持”来自不同地方，可信度不同。官方产品页与文档是入口。报价单和合同是承诺。销售邮件和会议纪要只是线索。博客、社区帖和第三方榜单不能当合同证据。每格都写“来源类型 + 链接/合同编号 + 核验日 + 负责人”。The same “supported” carries different weight depending on where it comes from. Official product pages and docs are entry points; quotes and contracts are commitments; sales emails and meeting notes are leads; blogs, community posts and third-party rankings are not contract evidence. Each cell records source type, link or contract ID, check date and owner.

把“未知”当成一个正式状态，而不是待办。未知的格子不能进入生产决策。要么补证据，要么写“不适用”，要么缩小范围。矩阵每季度复核一次，价格、区域、模型目录和条款都可能变。Treat “unknown” as a formal state, not a todo. An unknown cell cannot enter a production decision: get evidence, mark it not applicable, or reduce scope. Recheck the matrix quarterly; prices, regions, model catalogues and terms can change.

  • A 级：合同、订单、DPA、SLA 原文，带签署主体与日期。Level A: contract, order, DPA and SLA text with signing entity and date.
  • B 级：官方文档、控制台截图、官方公告，带链接与核验日。Level B: official docs, console screenshots and announcements with link and check date.
  • C 级：销售邮件、报价单、会议纪要，只能当线索。Level C: sales email, quote, meeting notes; leads only.
  • D 级：博客、社区、榜单、传闻，不作为合同证据。Level D: blogs, communities, rankings, rumours; not contract evidence.

8 · 失败分支：看起来“支持”，实际不能用8 · Failure branches: “supported” that does not work

中国区采购最常见的失败不是模型效果差，而是证据错位：官网说支持，合同不覆盖。区域写中国，模型不在售。私有化部署，推理仍调用外部 API。价格是国际站，发票是代理。数据留在境内，支持访问在境外。按现象倒查矩阵，不要只找销售确认。The most common China procurement failure is not model quality but mismatched evidence: the website says supported, the contract does not cover it; the region says China, the model is not offered; private deployment still calls an external API; the price is from an international site, the invoice from a reseller; data stays in-country, support access is abroad. Trace the symptom back to the matrix, not just to sales confirmation.

官网支持，合同不覆盖
优先怀疑：营销页与合同附件不一致
核对动作：把用途、区域、量写进合同再签Website says yes, contract says no
Suspect first: Marketing page vs contract schedule
Check: Put use, region and volume into the contract

中国区有站点，模型不在售
优先怀疑：站点区域与模型目录不同步
核对动作：控制台实际开通一次，截图留档China site, model absent
Suspect first: Site region vs model catalogue
Check: Open it in the console; keep a screenshot

私有化部署仍出网
优先怀疑：更新、遥测、支持通道
核对动作：抓包/防火墙验证，写进验收“Private” still egresses
Suspect first: Updates, telemetry, support channel
Check: Packet capture/firewall test; put in acceptance

价格低但账单高
优先怀疑：最低消费、出网、重试、税费
核对动作：按三个月用量做总成本Low price, high bill
Suspect first: Minimum, egress, retries, tax
Check: Three-month total cost

有发票但主体不对
优先怀疑：代理转售、合同主体
核对动作：核对签约主体与数据责任Invoice from wrong entity
Suspect first: Reseller, contract entity
Check: Check signing entity and data responsibility

数据境内，支持在境外
优先怀疑：跨境支持访问、工单附件
核对动作：要求脱敏、审批与审计记录Data local, support abroad
Suspect first: Cross-border support, ticket attachments
Check: Require redaction, approval and audit

许可允许商用，不允许再分发
优先怀疑：模型/权重/输出条款混读
核对动作：逐条读原文，书面澄清Commercial use, no redistribution
Suspect first: Model/weights/output terms conflated
Check: Read each text; get written clarification

SLA 有，但没有赔偿
优先怀疑：可用性承诺与 credit 条款
核对动作：看违约与终止条款SLA without remedy
Suspect first: Availability commitment and credits
Check: Read remedy and termination clauses

判断句：任何一格写“支持”但拿不出链接、合同条款或核验日，都按“未知”处理，不进入生产决策。Rule of thumb: if a cell says “supported” without a link, contract clause or check date, treat it as unknown and keep it out of the production decision.

9 · 最小落地路径9 · A minimum path to a verified matrix

先选一个真实任务和一类数据，不要一次覆盖全公司。用这张矩阵跑完一轮采购，再决定是否扩大供应商范围。Start with one real task and one data class, not the whole company. Run one procurement cycle through the matrix before widening the vendor set.

  • 定任务与数据分类：用途、数据级别、调用量、错误代价。Define task and data class: use, sensitivity, volume, cost of error.
  • 按交付形态分行：云 API、专属、私有化、本地权重、开源、集成商。Rows by delivery form: cloud API, dedicated, private, local weights, open source, integrator.
  • 每行只留 2–3 个候选，先打开官方入口，不先看第三方榜单。Keep 2–3 candidates per row; open official entries before third-party rankings.
  • 填六列：可用性、价格、驻留、许可、采购、支持。Fill six columns: availability, price, residency, licence, procurement, support.
  • 用 18 个采购问题问厂商，答案要带链接或条款。Ask the 18 procurement questions; answers need links or clauses.
  • 跑一个最小试用：真实数据、真实账号、真实账单。Run a minimum trial: real data, real account, real bill.
  • 让法务/安全/财务各看一列，不把判断压给采购一个人。Have legal/security/finance each own a column; do not leave it to procurement alone.
  • 把未知写进风险清单，设负责人和截止日。Put unknowns into the risk list with owner and deadline.
  • 签约后每季度复核价格、区域、模型目录与条款。After signing, recheck price, region, catalogue and terms quarterly.
完成标准不是“选了一家大厂”，而是：六列都有证据、未知有负责人、试用账单能复算、合同能覆盖你的用途。Done means six columns have evidence, unknowns have owners, the trial bill is recomputable and the contract covers your use — not that a big vendor was chosen.
