主题路线Topic route

数据与训练，按顺序找资料Data and training: resources in order

训练问题很少是「模型不够大」，更常见的是数据授权不清、训练集与测试集串了、污染没查、评测口径不一致。这条路线先处理数据，再谈训练与评测。Training problems are rarely just “the model is too small”; more often the data licence is unclear, train and test overlap, contamination is unchecked, or the evaluation basis differs. This route handles data first, then training and evaluation.

路线顺序不代表推荐排名；没有实测的能力对比、成本降幅与成功率，站群一律写未知。Route order is not a ranking; where no measured comparison, cost reduction or success rate exists, the sites record it as unknown.

边界Boundary

数据集卡只记录官方许可与已知 caveat，不替你判断「你的用途是否合规」；评测卡也不把厂商分数当独立复现。训练成本随硬件、批量、序列长度和利用率变化，本站只给口径与计算器，不给单一报价。Dataset cards record official licences and known caveats; they do not decide whether your use is compliant. Benchmark cards do not treat vendor scores as independent reproduction. Training cost varies with hardware, batch, sequence length and utilisation; the sites give the basis and a calculator, not a single quote.
