Corrections and source additions

Compose a correction draft

This site runs no product benchmarks; every conclusion hangs on an official source and a verification date. If a page has a wrong fact, a source that does not support it, a stale date or a translation mismatch, fill this in to get text you can paste into an issue in the content repository.

Nothing is sent from this page: no submission, no server, nothing stored beyond your own browser. Copy the draft, then decide whether to file it yourself.

Scope: say what that source covers and what it does not. A single link is not a re-verification of the whole page.
