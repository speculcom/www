Topic route

Images and design: resources in order

Start from what the image is for, not from a model: fix the use and size, then style and typography, then check asset rights and output format. This route puts tools, models, datasets and showcases in that order.

For People making product shots, posters, character sheets or campaign images You leave with A set of images with a spec and acceptance criteria Time 12 minutes to browse; about half a day for a six-image set

Read the field guide first: image spec, control, consistency, text, upscaling and rights →

Route order is not a ranking; where no measured comparison, cost reduction or success rate exists, the sites record it as unknown.

Boundary

The sites have not run a controlled same-prompt image comparison across models, and do not treat vendor samples as a quality ranking. Resolution, typography and small-text legibility must be re-run on your own assets; read the model and asset licences before commercial use.
