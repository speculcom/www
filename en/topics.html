Route order is not a ranking; where no measured comparison, cost reduction or success rate exists, the sites record it as unknown.

Field guides: when you have a concrete job

These guides do not start with definitions. They start with a concrete job: what you are making, the first step, what proves it is done and where to look when it breaks. Routes give order; guides give practice and acceptance.

  • Local models: how big a model can your GPU actually run?
  • Agents: write down what it may and may not do
  • AI video: from script to a finished clip you can deliver
  • Speech and voice: from a recording to a usable voice chain
  • Music generation: from a style brief to audio you can release
  • Image generation: from a brief to a set you can accept
  • 3D and digital humans: from concept art to an asset that imports
  • Data and datasets: from a pile of files to trainable material
  • AI safety and compliance: from data flow to a launch gate
  • Evaluation: what does this score actually tell you?
  • Office and business workflows: split a job into steps AI can take
  • Robotics and embodied AI: make one action repeatable first
  • Trust review: running, usable and accountable are separate checks
  • China availability: can you use it, what does it cost, where does data live?
