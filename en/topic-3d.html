Topic route

3D and digital humans: resources in order

3D and digital humans share three constraints: usable geometry, natural rigging and motion, and enough rendering and real-time performance. Fix the deliverable first (still, interactive model, short video, real-time avatar), then choose the generation path and pipeline.

For People handing a 3D asset to a game, film, shop or digital-human project You leave with An asset that imports, animates and holds the target frame rate Time 15 minutes to browse; 1–3 days for one asset

Route order is not a ranking; where no measured comparison, cost reduction or success rate exists, the sites record it as unknown.

Boundary

No controlled comparison of geometry accuracy, rigging quality or real-time frame rate across 3D/avatar pipelines has been run; a showcase proves an output exists, not that it is production-ready. Topology, UVs, materials and driving stability must be re-tested on your assets.
