Build robots with AI: resources in order

This page does not restate concepts or rank tools. It puts published material into the order in which a robot gets built: set the task and runtime, find the software stack, models and directories, then check the result. Conditions, sources and scope stay on the linked pages.

For People building a robot demo, experiment or small deployment You leave with A route from body and simulation to real-world repetition Time 10 minutes to browse; 1–2 weeks to make one action repeatable

Where the sites record no measured comparison, cost reduction or success rate, this page does not add one.
