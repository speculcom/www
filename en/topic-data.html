Topic route

Data and training: resources in order

Training problems are rarely just “the model is too small”; more often the data licence is unclear, train and test overlap, contamination is unchecked, or the evaluation basis differs. This route handles data first, then training and evaluation.

For People cleaning, labelling, splitting or preparing training data You leave with A reproducible data card and a rights checklist Time 12 minutes to browse; about a day for a small batch

Route order is not a ranking; where no measured comparison, cost reduction or success rate exists, the sites record it as unknown.

Boundary

Dataset cards record official licences and known caveats; they do not decide whether your use is compliant. Benchmark cards do not treat vendor scores as independent reproduction. Training cost varies with hardware, batch, sequence length and utilisation; the sites give the basis and a calculator, not a single quote.
