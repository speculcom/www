Safety and compliance: resources in order

Safety is not one added sentence: define the threat model, look at evaluations and incident records, then implement permissions, data handling and logs. Compliance is not one checklist either; separate the applicable rules, the evidence and the owner.

For People connecting AI to real business and facing review You leave with A data-flow map and a pre-launch evidence checklist Time 15 minutes to browse; about a day for a minimum review

Route order is not a ranking; where no measured comparison, cost reduction or success rate exists, the sites record it as unknown.

  • Manager route: who signs, who reviews, how incidents are traced
  • Review checklist and release gates

Boundary

This is not legal advice and does not issue a compliance conclusion for any organisation; statutes, regulator interpretations and contracts govern. Safety scores hold only for their threat model and test set; one benchmark cannot prove “safe overall”.
