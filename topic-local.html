这一页不重复讲概念，也不给硬件排名。它把全站已经写好的资料按在本机跑起一个模型串起来：先算显存与量化，再选设备与运行时，最后核对速度、成本与失败分支。具体条件、来源和适用范围都在被链接的页面里。This page does not restate concepts or rank hardware. It puts published material into the order in which a model runs locally: work out memory and quantisation, choose the device and runtime, then check speed, cost and failure branches. Conditions, sources and scope stay on the linked pages.
