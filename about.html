关于 SpeculAbout Specul

一个把学 AI、选工具、看模型、读论文、试作品接在一起的站群。不是媒体，不是排行榜，也不是课程售卖页。A network of sites that connects learning AI, choosing tools, reading models, browsing papers and trying real work. Not a media outlet, not a leaderboard, and not a course sales page.

我们想解决什么What we are trying to solve

AI 信息不缺，缺的是能落到一个具体决定的信息。普通用户想用好 AI，通常卡在四个问题：这东西是什么、我该用哪个、它靠不靠谱、出了问题找谁。Specul 的十个站分别回答这四类问题，并用统一的出处、日期和边界口径把它们串起来。There is no shortage of AI information; what is scarce is information that lands on a specific decision. People trying to use AI usually get stuck on four questions: what is this, which one should I use, can I trust it, and who do I ask when it breaks. The ten Specul sites answer those four questions and connect them with one standard for sources, dates and limits.

所以我们不做「每天 50 条快讯」，也不做「最强模型排行榜」。我们做的是可复核的档案：每个判断尽量带官方来源、核验日期和「它没主张什么」。So we do not publish fifty news flashes a day, and we do not rank the “strongest model”. We build archives you can check: every judgement carries an official source where possible, a verification date, and a clear note on what it does not claim.

我们相信的三件事Three things we believe

先给边界，再给结论。每个产品、模型、论文都写「适合谁 / 不适合谁 / 没证明什么」。没有边界的推荐，等于广告。Limits before conclusions. Every product, model and paper says who it suits, who it does not, and what it has not shown. A recommendation without limits is an advertisement.

来源比观点重要。能链到官方文档、规范、论文或仓库，就链；链不到就标「待核」。宁可留一个空格，也不编一个看起来合理的数字。Sources matter more than opinions. If it can be linked to official docs, a spec, a paper or a repository, we link it. If not, it is marked “to verify”. We would rather leave a blank than invent a plausible number.

日期是内容的一部分。AI 产品三个月就换一代。每个档案带核验日期；过期不是删掉重写，而是标出来让你自己判断。Dates are part of the content. AI products turn over every few months. Every profile carries a verification date. Out-of-date entries are marked, not silently rewritten, so you can judge for yourself.

我们不做什么What we do not do

不做付费排名，不做「最强模型」榜单，不卖课，不把未核实的参数写成规格，不把「公开可下载」写成「可商用」，不把模型输出当成事实。我们也不提供投资、法律或医疗建议。We do not sell rankings, publish a “strongest model” list, sell courses, turn unverified numbers into specs, turn “publicly downloadable” into “commercially usable”, or treat model output as fact. We do not give investment, legal or medical advice.
