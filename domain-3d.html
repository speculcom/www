领域深度 · 3D 与数字人Field guide · 3D and digital humans

3D 与数字人：从概念图到能进引擎的资产3D and digital humans: from concept art to an asset that imports

你拿到一个看着不错的 3D 模型，先别急着交付。把它拖进 Unity，可能尺寸不对。绑上骨骼，弯一下肘就塌。放进手机，帧率掉一半。它最后能不能用，看三件事：进得去、动得起来、跑得动。先写清楚它要在哪用，再决定哪些步骤让 AI 生成、哪些必须人工收尾。You have a 3D model that looks good. Do not deliver it yet. Import it into Unity and the scale may be wrong; rig it and the elbow collapses; put it on a phone and the frame rate halves. Three things decide whether it works: it imports, it animates, and it runs. Write where it will be used before deciding which steps AI can generate and which need a human finish.

拿一个具体活走一遍One concrete job

假设一个具体活：把一只咖啡机模型放进 Unity，做 12 秒产品短片，手机 60 帧，需要开盖动作和一条品牌色灯带。先按这个交付写验收表。Take one concrete job: a coffee machine in Unity for a 12-second product shot, 60 fps on a phone, with a lid-opening animation and a brand-colour light strip. Write the acceptance sheet for that delivery.

1 · 四种交付形态，四套验收表1 · Four deliverables, four acceptance tables

“做一个 3D 模型”不是交付描述。静帧要的是轮廓、材质和灯光。可交互模型要的是能导入引擎、能碰撞、能进帧预算。镜头画面要的是动作、镜头和连续性。实时数字人要的是口型、表情、延迟和长时间稳定。四者的资产结构、面数预算与验收证据完全不同。“Make a 3D model” is not a delivery spec. A still needs silhouette, material and lighting. An interactive model must import into an engine, support collision and fit a frame budget. A shot needs motion, camera and continuity. A real-time avatar needs lip sync, expression, latency and long-run stability. Their asset structure, polygon budget and acceptance evidence differ completely.

先把交付写成一句可核对的话：在什么设备、什么引擎、什么帧率、看多近、动多远、由谁验收。写不出来，就会在最后才争论“这个模型能不能用”。Write the deliverable as one checkable sentence: on which device, in which engine, at which frame rate, viewed from how close, moving how far, accepted by whom. If that sentence cannot be written, the argument about “whether this model is usable” will only start at the end.

2 · 几何从哪来：生成、重建、扫描、手工2 · Where geometry comes from: generation, reconstruction, scanning, manual

生成模型擅长给“第一版形状”，不擅长保证拓扑、UV、法线和部件关系。图像或文本生成常见的失败不是“不像”，而是背面凭空补、部件粘在一起、网格有洞、贴图已经烘进颜色。重建与扫描能得到真实物体，但会带来噪声、缺面和比例漂移。手工建模最慢，却最适合需要动画和长期维护的资产。Generative models are good at a first shape, not at guaranteeing topology, UVs, normals or part relationships. The usual failure is not “unrealistic” but an invented backside, fused parts, holes and colour baked into textures. Reconstruction and scanning capture a real object, but bring noise, missing surfaces and scale drift. Manual modelling is slowest, yet best for assets that must animate and be maintained.

选择标准不是“哪个生成得最像”，而是“这个资产要不要动、要不要进引擎、要不要反复修改”。要动的资产，拓扑和绑定优先。只看一张图的资产，轮廓和材质优先。真实场景，尺度和缺面优先。Choose by “will this asset animate, enter an engine and be revised”, not by “which generator looks best”. Animated assets prioritise topology and rigging; single-image assets prioritise silhouette and material; real scenes prioritise scale and missing surfaces.

3 · 拓扑、UV 与法线：决定能不能动、能不能贴3 · Topology, UVs and normals: whether it can move and texture

生成网格看起来面很多，不等于拓扑合格。非流形边、孤立部件、极点位置不对，会让绑定一弯就塌，让物理模拟穿透，让 3D 打印切片失败。UV 重叠会让两张贴图互相污染。texel density 不一致会让近景糊、远景浪费。法线切线不统一会在引擎里出现硬边和黑面。A dense generated mesh is not a valid topology. Non-manifold edges, loose parts and badly placed poles collapse under deformation, break simulation and fail slicing for printing. Overlapping UVs contaminate textures; inconsistent texel density blurs close-ups and wastes resolution at distance; mismatched normals and tangents create hard edges and black faces in an engine.

检查顺序要固定：先看尺度和朝向，再看非流形与孤立部件，再看面数和边流，最后看 UV、texel density、法线与切线。顺序反过来，会在错误的比例上做精细拓扑，白做。Use a fixed order: scale and orientation, then non-manifold edges and loose parts, then polygon count and edge flow, finally UVs, texel density, normals and tangents. Reverse the order and you will retopologise a wrongly scaled asset for nothing.

4 · 材质与贴图：先定材质模型，再定分辨率4 · Materials and textures: fix the material model before resolution

PBR 材质不是“多贴几张图”。base color 管颜色，roughness 与 metallic 管反射，normal 管表面起伏，AO 管遮蔽，emissive 管自发光，opacity 管透明。颜色贴图通常按 sRGB 读，数据贴图按线性读。把 roughness 当颜色贴图处理，反射会整体错。A PBR material is not “more texture maps”. Base colour carries albedo, roughness and metallic drive reflection, normal carries surface detail, AO carries occlusion, emissive carries emission and opacity carries transparency. Colour maps are usually read as sRGB, data maps as linear; treating roughness as a colour map throws off reflection across the asset.

分辨率由观看距离和 texel density 决定，不由“越大越好”决定。4K 贴图贴在指甲盖上，是浪费。512 贴图铺满一面墙，会糊。透明、双面、折射和贴花还会改变渲染排序与性能，必须在目标引擎里看，而不是只看建模软件。Resolution follows viewing distance and texel density, not “bigger is better”. A 4K map on a fingernail is waste; a 512 map across a wall is blur. Transparency, double-sided surfaces, refraction and decals also change sorting and performance; judge them in the target engine, not only in the modelling tool.

5 · 绑定与动作：拓扑不对，权重救不了5 · Rigging and motion: weights cannot rescue bad topology

绑定不是“套一根骨骼”。人体要肩、肘、膝、脊柱和手指的旋转轴。面部要 blendshape 或骨骼组合。道具要区分刚体与可变形部分。场景要区分静态碰撞体与可动件。关节位置、骨骼朝向和模型比例不一致，权重画得再细也会在极限姿势下塌陷。Rigging is not “add one skeleton”. A human needs shoulder, elbow, knee, spine and finger rotation axes; a face needs blendshapes or a bone set; a prop separates rigid and deformable parts; a scene separates static colliders from movable parts. Wrong joint placement, bone orientation or body proportions collapse under extreme poses no matter how carefully weights are painted.

动作来源可以捕获、可以手 K、可以视频驱动或重定向。每一种都要做“极限姿势测试”：抬臂、深蹲、扭腰、张嘴、闭眼、快速转头。只看一段自然站姿，无法证明绑定可用。Motion can be captured, keyframed, video-driven or retargeted. Each needs extreme-pose tests: arm raise, deep squat, torso twist, mouth open, eyes closed, fast head turn. A natural idle pose does not prove a rig.

6 · 渲染与实时：先锁设备和帧率6 · Rendering and real time: lock the device and frame rate first

离线渲染可以拿时间换质量。实时渲染必须先把帧预算写死。60 fps 每帧 16.7 ms，30 fps 每帧 33.3 ms，90 Hz 的 XR 每帧 11.1 ms，而且这些时间还要分给应用逻辑、物理、动画、UI 和音频。只看“编辑器里能转”，不能证明目标设备能跑。Offline rendering trades time for quality; real time starts by fixing a frame budget. At 60 fps a frame is 16.7 ms, at 30 fps 33.3 ms and at 90 Hz XR 11.1 ms; that budget also pays for application logic, physics, animation, UI and audio. “It turns in the editor” does not prove the target device runs it.

先测四个量：draw call、三角面、纹理内存、每帧 CPU/GPU 时间。再查过度绘制、蒙皮骨骼数、透明材质和着色器复杂度。LOD 不是最后补的优化，而是资产规范的一部分：每级 LOD 的面数、材质和切换距离要事先写。Measure four numbers first: draw calls, triangles, texture memory and CPU/GPU time per frame. Then inspect overdraw, bone count for skinning, transparent materials and shader complexity. LOD is not a final optimisation; it is part of the asset spec, with polygon count, materials and switch distance written per level.

先写的预算：11.1 ms / 帧，双眼渲染
先测什么：延迟、重投影、晕动
常见瓶颈：分辨率、透明、骨骼数XR 90 Hz
Budget to write: 11.1 ms per frame, stereo
Measure first: Latency, reprojection, motion sickness
Typical bottleneck: Resolution, transparency, bone count

网页 / WebGL
先写的预算：下载体积、显存、首帧
先测什么：加载时间、上下文丢失
常见瓶颈：贴图过多、着色器编译Web / WebGL
Budget to write: Download size, VRAM, first frame
Measure first: Load time, context loss
Typical bottleneck: Too many textures, shader compilation

先锁目标设备和帧率，再谈面数与贴图。“在编辑器里能转”不是实时验收。Lock the target device and frame rate before arguing polygon count and textures. An editor turntable is not a real-time acceptance test.

7 · 数字人专项：脸、口型、眼睛和长时间稳定7 · Digital humans: face, lip sync, eyes and long-run stability

数字人最容易在“脸”上翻车。静态像，动起来可能眼皮不眨、眼球不追、嘴角过冲、头发穿模。音频驱动口型时，还要对齐音素、重音和停顿。一个 10 秒演示可以靠挑片段过关，连续讲 10 分钟才会暴露漂移和疲劳。Digital humans fail most often at the face. A good still can blink wrong, track eyes poorly, overshoot mouth shapes or make hair intersect the skin. Audio-driven lip sync must align phonemes, stress and pauses. A ten-second demo can be curated; ten minutes of speech exposes drift and fatigue.

把数字人拆成头、脸、眼、发、身体、声音六层验收。头看比例与转动，脸看表情范围，眼看注视与眨眼，发看穿模与物理，身体看手势与坐姿，声音看口型、音色和延迟。任何一层用“整体感觉”代替，都会在客户现场返工。Split the avatar into six acceptance layers: head, face, eyes, hair, body and voice. Head checks proportion and rotation; face checks expression range; eyes check gaze and blink; hair checks intersection and physics; body checks gesture and seated pose; voice checks lip sync, timbre and latency. Replacing any layer with “overall feel” causes rework at the client demo.

头部 / 比例
要做什么：颅骨、五官、颈肩连接
验收：多角度转动不穿、不变形
风险：近景比例失真Head / proportion
What to build: Skull, features, neck-shoulder join
Acceptance: No intersection or deformation through rotation
Risk: Proportion breaks in close-up

表情
要做什么：blendshape 或骨骼组合
验收：喜怒惊厌与微表情不过冲
风险：表情像面具Expression
What to build: Blendshape or bone set
Acceptance: Emotion and micro-expression do not overshoot
Risk: Mask-like expression

眼睛 / 眨眼
要做什么：注视、瞳孔、眼睑
验收：跟随镜头、自然眨眼
风险：死盯、眨眼机械Eyes / blink
What to build: Gaze, pupil, lids
Acceptance: Follows camera, natural blink
Risk: Staring, mechanical blink

头发 / 服装
要做什么：发束、碰撞、布料
验收：快速转头不穿模
风险：穿脸、抖动Hair / cloth
What to build: Hair strands, collision, cloth
Acceptance: No intersection on fast head turn
Risk: Intersects face, jitters

口型 / 声音
要做什么：音素到视素、重音、停顿
验收：长句不错位、延迟可测
风险：音画不同步Lip sync / voice
What to build: Phonemes to visemes, stress, pauses
Acceptance: Long sentences stay aligned, latency measurable
Risk: Audio and video drift

长时间运行
要做什么：内存、温度、降级策略
验收：连续 30 分钟不崩
风险：越跑越慢、显存泄漏Long run
What to build: Memory, heat, degradation
Acceptance: Thirty minutes without a crash
Risk: Slows down, VRAM leak

数字人最容易被“脸”骗。先看口型、眨眼、头部转动和长时间稳定性。A digital human is easiest to fake at the face. Check lip sync, blink, head turn and long-run stability first.

8 · 导出与导入：格式对了，场景还可能错8 · Export and import: the format can be right and the scene still wrong

glTF 适合网页与实时，USD 适合影视与场景组装，FBX 仍常见于 DCC 交换。它们对单位、轴、材质、动画和骨骼的约定不同。导出成功不等于导入成功：比例可能差 100 倍，材质可能丢，动画帧率可能被重采样，骨骼朝向可能翻转。glTF suits web and real time, USD suits film and scene assembly, FBX remains common for DCC exchange; each treats units, axes, materials, animation and skeletons differently. A successful export is not a successful import: scale can be off by 100, materials can drop, animation can be resampled and bone orientation can flip.

验收要在目标引擎里做，不在导出对话框里做。导入后检查尺度、朝向、材质、动画、碰撞、LOD、命名和文件大小。再存一份可回滚的源资产与导出记录。Accept in the target engine, not in the export dialog. After import, check scale, orientation, materials, animation, collision, LOD, naming and file size; keep a rollback-ready source asset and export record.

单位与比例
为什么：不同工具默认单位不同
怎么验：在空场景放已知尺寸人形 / 立方体Units and scale
Why: DCC tools default to different units
How to verify: Place a known-size human or cube in an empty scene

轴与朝向
为什么：Y-up / Z-up 与前后轴不同
怎么验：从四个方向看，检查正面与地面Axes and facing
Why: Y-up / Z-up and forward axes differ
How to verify: View from four sides, check front and ground

材质转换
为什么：PBR 通道可能被简化或丢失
怎么验：逐通道对比源材质与目标材质Material conversion
Why: PBR channels may be simplified or dropped
How to verify: Compare each channel between source and target

动画与帧率
为什么：重采样会改变节奏
怎么验：逐帧对比关键姿势与总时长Animation and frame rate
Why: Resampling changes timing
How to verify: Compare key poses and duration frame by frame

碰撞与 LOD
为什么：导入器不会自动生成
怎么验：走一遍、撞一次、切三级 LODCollision and LOD
Why: Importers do not create them automatically
How to verify: Walk, collide and switch three LODs

命名与清单
为什么：资产一多就找不到
怎么验：按规范命名，附源文件、贴图、许可Naming and manifest
Why: Assets get lost at scale
How to verify: Name by spec, attach source, textures and licence

导出不是最后一步。导入目标引擎并跑一遍，才算导出成功。Export is not the last step. Import into the target engine and run it before calling the export successful.

9 · 权利：模型、训练数据、参考物、真人四层分开9 · Rights: model, training data, reference and person are four layers

3D 资产的权利至少四层：生成模型与服务条款、训练数据与风格来源、输入参考物（照片、扫描、其他模型）、输出资产的使用范围。商业模型许可不等于参考照片可以商用。拥有输出文件不等于可以拿真人扫描做数字人。CC0 的模型可以改，但不代表里面的商标、角色或建筑可以随便用。A 3D asset carries at least four rights layers: the model and service terms, training data and style sources, input references (photos, scans, other models), and permitted use of the output. A commercial model licence does not make a reference photo commercial; owning the output file does not make a real-person scan usable as an avatar; a CC0 model can be modified, but trademarks, characters or buildings inside it are not automatically free.

真人扫描、数字人、声音克隆还要同意、范围、期限、撤回与标识。把同意书、参考来源、生成记录、编辑记录和发布范围放进同一个资产清单。没有清单，后面无法证明你凭什么用。Real-person scans, digital humans and voice clones also need consent, scope, duration, withdrawal and labelling. Keep consent, references, generation logs, edit records and publication scope in one asset manifest; without it, you cannot prove the basis for use later.

  • 给每个资产建一行清单：来源、作者、许可、参考物、同意书、生成工具、编辑记录、发布范围。Give every asset one manifest row: source, author, licence, references, consent, generation tool, edits and publication scope.
  • 区分“个人使用”“商业发布”“再训练”“再分发”，不要把一次授权当成全部用途。Separate personal use, commercial publication, retraining and redistribution; do not treat one permission as all uses.
  • 真人肖像、声音、姓名、商标、建筑外观、角色形象分别核对，不能用一个“CC0” blanket 结论。Check likeness, voice, name, trademark, building exterior and character separately; one “CC0” conclusion cannot cover them all.
  • 合成内容按平台与适用规则标识。保留可撤回与下架的路径。Label synthetic media according to platform and applicable rules; keep a withdrawal and takedown path.
权利不是一句“可以商用”。把用途、地域、期限、标识和证据分开写。Rights are not one line saying “commercial use allowed”. Separate use, territory, duration, labelling and evidence.

10 · 成本：按验收通过的资产算，不按生成次数算10 · Cost: price accepted assets, not generation attempts

3D 成本最容易算错的地方，是把“生成一次”当“做完一个”。真实成本包括生成尝试、清理与重拓扑、UV 与贴图、绑定与动作、渲染与转码、存储与版本、验收与返工。生成次数越多，不等于资产越便宜。有时只是把返工推迟到后面。The common costing error is treating one generation as one finished asset. Real cost includes generation attempts, cleanup and retopology, UVs and textures, rigging and motion, rendering and transcoding, storage and versions, acceptance and rework. More generations do not make an asset cheaper; they may only postpone the rework.

报价单位建议用“验收通过的资产”或“验收通过的镜头秒”，并把返工轮次、源文件交付、独家/非独家、二次修改写进合同。按 token、按张、按次报价都无法覆盖后面的手工段。Quote per accepted asset or accepted shot second, and write rework rounds, source-file delivery, exclusivity and later revisions into the contract. Per-token, per-image or per-run pricing cannot cover the manual stages.

生成尝试
单位：每次 / 每批
为什么会失控：反复抽卡、没有停止条件
控制办法：先定批量上限与通过标准Generation attempts
Unit: Per run / batch
Why it runs away: Endless sampling without a stop rule
Control: Set a batch cap and pass criteria first

清理与重拓扑
单位：小时 / 资产
为什么会失控：生成网格不可直接动画
控制办法：先定面数、边流与 UV 目标Cleanup and retopology
Unit: Hours per asset
Why it runs away: Generated mesh cannot animate directly
Control: Fix polygon, edge-flow and UV targets

绑定与动作
单位：小时 / 骨架
为什么会失控：每套骨架重做、重定向失败
控制办法：复用骨架与命名规范Rig and motion
Unit: Hours per rig
Why it runs away: New skeleton per asset, retarget fails
Control: Reuse skeletons and naming rules

贴图与材质
单位：张 / 材质
为什么会失控：4K/8K 无预算、通道打包错
控制办法：按 texel density 定分辨率Textures and materials
Unit: Maps per material
Why it runs away: Unbudgeted 4K/8K, wrong packing
Control: Set resolution from texel density

渲染与转码
单位：帧 / GPU 小时
为什么会失控：采样过高、返工重渲
控制办法：先出低分辨率测试帧Render and transcode
Unit: Frame / GPU hour
Why it runs away: Oversampling, re-render after fixes
Control: Test at low resolution first

存储与版本
单位：GB / 版本
为什么会失控：每版都存全量、贴图重复
控制办法：增量版本与保留策略Storage and versions
Unit: GB per version
Why it runs away: Full copies per version, duplicate textures
Control: Incremental versions and retention

验收与返工
单位：轮 / 资产
为什么会失控：标准不清、口头修改
控制办法：验收表 + 每轮书面清单Acceptance and rework
Unit: Rounds per asset
Why it runs away: Unclear criteria, verbal changes
Control: Acceptance table plus written round notes

报价按“验收通过的资产”算，不按“生成次数”算。Quote per accepted asset, not per generation attempt.

11 · 失败分支：先定位在哪一段，再决定换不换工具11 · Failure branches: locate the stage before swapping tools

3D 故障往往在导出后才暴露，但根因在前面。先按“几何、材质、绑定、实时、导出、权利”分段定位，再决定是修资产、修管线还是换工具。不要用“再生成一次”掩盖一个比例或法线问题。3D failures often surface after export, but the cause sits earlier. Locate the stage — geometry, material, rig, real time, export, rights — before deciding whether to fix the asset, the pipeline or the tool. Do not hide a scale or normal problem behind another generation.

模型有洞、漂浮件
先查：非流形边、连通组件
常见根因：生成未清理
处理：补洞、合并、重拓扑Holes and floating parts
Check first: Non-manifold edges, connected components
Likely cause: Generation not cleaned
Fix: Fill, merge, retopologise

进引擎变大 / 变小
先查：单位、轴、导入设置
常见根因：源场景单位不一致
处理：统一单位，放参考物复验Wrong scale in engine
Check first: Units, axes, import settings
Likely cause: Source scene units differ
Fix: Unify units, recheck against reference

一弯就塌
先查：关节位置、边流、权重
常见根因：拓扑不适合变形
处理：重做关节环线，再刷权重Collapses when bent
Check first: Joint placement, edge flow, weights
Likely cause: Topology unsuitable for deformation
Fix: Rebuild joint loops, then weights

材质发灰 / 过亮
先查：颜色空间、PBR 通道
常见根因：sRGB / linear 用错
处理：按通道重设，目标引擎复核Washed-out or bright material
Check first: Colour space, PBR channels
Likely cause: Wrong sRGB / linear assignment
Fix: Reset per channel, verify in engine

实时掉帧
先查：draw call、面数、纹理、蒙皮
常见根因：预算未定或 LOD 缺失
处理：先砍 draw call 与贴图，再做 LODFrame drops
Check first: Draw calls, triangles, textures, skinning
Likely cause: No budget or missing LODs
Fix: Cut draw calls and textures first, then LOD

数字人口型漂移
先查：音素、视素、帧率、延迟
常见根因：音画时钟不一致
处理：对齐时钟，长句复测Lip-sync drift
Check first: Phonemes, visemes, frame rate, latency
Likely cause: Audio and video clocks differ
Fix: Align clocks, retest long sentences

授权说不清
先查：模型条款、参考物、真人同意
常见根因：只有生成记录，没有来源链
处理：补清单，换掉不可用素材Unclear rights
Check first: Model terms, references, consent
Likely cause: Generation logs without provenance
Fix: Complete the manifest, replace unusable assets

先定位段，再换工具。换工具不解决比例、拓扑和授权。Locate the stage before swapping tools. A new tool does not fix scale, topology or rights.

12 · 一条最小路径：从交付句到可验收资产12 · A minimum path: from delivery sentence to accepted asset

下面这条路径适用于一件要进引擎或要拍进镜头的资产。每一步都留下证据，下一才能开始。This path fits one asset that must enter an engine or appear in a shot. Each step leaves evidence before the next begins.

  • 写交付句：目标设备、引擎、帧率、观看距离、动作范围、验收人。Write the delivery sentence: target device, engine, frame rate, viewing distance, motion range and acceptor.
  • 收参考与权利清单：照片、扫描、模型来源、许可、真人同意、商标检查。Collect references and a rights manifest: photos, scans, model sources, licences, consent, trademark checks.
  • 选几何来源：生成、重建、扫描或手工。先做低模草稿，不急着上高模。Choose the geometry source: generate, reconstruct, scan or model manually; start with a low-poly blockout.
  • 清几何：统一单位、朝向、原点，补洞，去孤立件，定面数预算。Clean geometry: unify units, orientation and pivot, fill holes, remove loose parts, set the polygon budget.
  • 重拓扑、UV、法线与切线：按变形与观看距离定边流和 texel density。Retopologise, unwrap, and fix normals and tangents around deformation and viewing distance.
  • 做材质与贴图：按 PBR 通道、颜色空间和分辨率预算逐项验收。Build materials and textures; accept each PBR channel, colour space and resolution budget.
  • 绑定与动作：关节、权重、blendshape、重定向。做极限姿势测试。Rig and animate: joints, weights, blendshapes, retargeting; run extreme-pose tests.
  • 导出并导入目标引擎：查尺度、材质、动画、碰撞、LOD、帧率。Export and import into the target engine: check scale, materials, animation, collision, LOD and frame rate.
  • 打包资产清单：源文件、贴图、许可、同意、生成与编辑记录、验收表。Package the asset manifest: source files, textures, licences, consent, generation and edit records, acceptance table.
  • 交付并留回滚：保存可复现版本，记录未解决项与下一轮修改条件。Deliver with a rollback: keep a reproducible version, record unresolved items and conditions for the next revision.
最小路径不是“最快路径”。它是能复现、能验收、能回滚的最短闭环。The minimum path is not the fastest path; it is the shortest reproducible, acceptable and rollback-ready loop.
