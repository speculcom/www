主题路线Topic route

做图与设计，按顺序找资料Images and design: resources in order

从「要一张什么图」开始，而不是先打开某个模型：先定用途与尺寸，再看风格与文字排版，最后核对素材授权和输出格式。这条路线把工具、模型、数据集与作品页按这个顺序串起来。Start from what the image is for, not from a model: fix the use and size, then style and typography, then check asset rights and output format. This route puts tools, models, datasets and showcases in that order.

适合谁For 要做电商图、海报、角色设定或产品图的人People making product shots, posters, character sheets or campaign images 读完能拿到You leave with 一套有规格、有验收的图A set of images with a spec and acceptance criteria 大概多久Time 12 分钟浏览；做 6 张一套约半天12 minutes to browse; about half a day for a six-image set

先读领域长文：图像规格、控制、一致性、文字、放大与授权Read the field guide first: image spec, control, consistency, text, upscaling and rights →

路线顺序不代表推荐排名；没有实测的能力对比、成本降幅与成功率，站群一律写未知。Route order is not a ranking; where no measured comparison, cost reduction or success rate exists, the sites record it as unknown.

边界Boundary

站群没有做过「同一提示词下多模型出图」的受控对比，也不把厂商样片当质量排名。分辨率、文字排版和小字可读性必须用你自己的素材复跑；商用前逐条读模型与素材许可。The sites have not run a controlled same-prompt image comparison across models, and do not treat vendor samples as a quality ranking. Resolution, typography and small-text legibility must be re-run on your own assets; read the model and asset licences before commercial use.
