纠错与来源补充Corrections and source additions

生成一份纠错草稿Compose a correction draft

这站不做产品实测，写的结论都能在官方来源里找到，每页也标了资料时间。如果你发现某页的事实、来源、日期或翻译有问题，可以在这里把要说明的几点填齐，生成一段可以直接贴进内容仓议题的文本。This site runs no product benchmarks; every conclusion hangs on an official source and a verification date. If a page has a wrong fact, a source that does not support it, a stale date or a translation mismatch, fill this in to get text you can paste into an issue in the content repository.

这个页面不发送任何内容：不提交表单、不写服务端、不存到你的浏览器之外。复制之后由你自己决定要不要提交。Nothing is sent from this page: no submission, no server, nothing stored beyond your own browser. Copy the draft, then decide whether to file it yourself.

范围说明：写清这条依据覆盖哪一点，不覆盖什么。本站不会因为一条链接就把整页事实判为已复核。Scope: say what that source covers and what it does not. A single link is not a re-verification of the whole page.
