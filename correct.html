本站不跑产品实测，结论都挂在官方来源与核验日期上。如果你发现某页的事实、来源、日期或翻译有问题，可以在这里把要说明的几点填齐，生成一段可以直接贴进内容仓议题的文本。This site runs no product benchmarks; every conclusion hangs on an official source and a verification date. If a page has a wrong fact, a source that does not support it, a stale date or a translation mismatch, fill this in to get text you can paste into an issue in the content repository.

这个页面不发送任何内容：不提交表单、不写服务端、不存到你的浏览器之外。复制之后由你自己决定要不要提交。Nothing is sent from this page: no submission, no server, nothing stored beyond your own browser. Copy the draft, then decide whether to file it yourself.
