领域深度 · 数据与语料Field guide · Data and datasets

数据与语料：从一堆文件到能训练的语料Data and datasets: from a pile of files to trainable material

你从网盘里拖出 20 万条对话，觉得“数据够了”。真开训才发现：有的客户没授权，有的字段带手机号，训练集和测试集撞了同一批样本，出了问题还删不干净。数据能不能用，不看大小，看它能不能回答：从哪来、凭什么用、怎么处理、怎么复现、怎么删。You pull 200,000 conversations from a shared drive and call it “enough data”. Training starts and the problems appear: some customers never consented, some fields contain phone numbers, train and test share the same samples, and nobody can delete a bad batch cleanly. Whether data is usable is not a size question. It has to answer: where it came from, why you may use it, how it was processed, how to reproduce it and how to delete it.

适合谁For 要清洗、标注、切分或准备训练语料的人Anyone cleaning, labelling, splitting or preparing training data 读完能拿到You leave with 一张数据卡、一张权利清单，和一条从用途句到可复现版本的路径A data card, a rights checklist and a path from the use sentence to a reproducible version 大概多久Time 先读 12 分钟；整理一小批数据约 1 天12 minutes to read; about a day for a small batch 最后核对 2026-11-25Last checked 25 Nov 2026

本页是 Specul 的数据生产判断框架，不是法律意见，也不是数据集或许可的排名。具体用途、许可与合规结论以授权文件、合同、适用法律与原始来源为准。This is Specul’s data-production framework, not legal advice or a ranking of datasets or licences. Specific use, licensing and compliance conclusions remain with the authorisation documents, contracts, applicable law and original sources.

如果你只有 5 分钟If you only have five minutes
  1. 先写用途句：这份数据到底要训练什么。Write the use sentence first: what exactly is this data training?
  2. 版权、合同、隐私、肖像分开核，不能合成一个“有授权”。Check copyright, contract, privacy and likeness separately; do not collapse them into “licensed”.
  3. 训练、验证、测试要按来源切，不能只随机切。Split train, validation and test by source, not just randomly.

完整版从下面开始；表格是查的时候用，不用背。The full guide starts below. Use the tables for reference; you do not need to memorise them.

拿一个具体活走一遍One concrete job

假设一个具体活：用 2,000 条客服对话训一个工单分类器，只分“退款、物流、产品”三类。先写来源、许可、字段、切分和删除方式。Take one concrete job: 2,000 support conversations to train a ticket classifier with three labels: refund, shipping and product. Write the source, licence, fields, split and deletion method first.

一条数据能不能用，不看它有多大，看它能不能回答：从哪来、凭什么用、怎么处理、怎么复现、出了问题怎么删。Whether a record is usable is not a matter of size. It must answer where it came from, on what basis it is used, how it was processed, how it reproduces and how it can be removed.

1 · 先定用途：预训练、微调、偏好、评测、RAG 要的数据不一样1 · Fix the purpose: pretraining, fine-tuning, preference, evaluation and RAG need different data

同一个“数据不够”可能指五种不同问题：预训练缺规模与多样性，微调缺任务示例，偏好数据缺成对比较，评测集缺区分度，RAG 缺可检索且可引用的文档。五种用途的最小字段、质量门槛和风险完全不同。拿评测集去微调，拿网页语料去做 RAG，都会在最后才发现不可用。“Not enough data” can mean five different problems: pretraining lacks scale and diversity, fine-tuning lacks task examples, preference data lacks paired comparisons, evaluation lacks discrimination, RAG lacks retrievable and citable documents. Their minimum fields, quality bars and risks differ completely. Fine-tuning on an evaluation set or building RAG from raw web text only reveals the mismatch at the end.

用途要写成“输入—输出—判定”的句子。例如“给定一段客服对话，输出问题分类与是否升级，人工按 12 类标签判定”。写不出输入输出和判定，标注员只能自由发挥，质量无法复现。Write the purpose as an input–output–judgement sentence. For example: “given a customer-support dialogue, output the issue category and whether to escalate; a human judges against 12 labels.” Without input, output and judgement, annotators improvise and quality cannot reproduce.

2 · 权利不是一列：版权、合同、隐私、肖像分开核2 · Rights are not one column: copyright, contract, privacy and likeness are separate

“公开可访问”不等于“可以训练、可以商用、可以再分发”。网页内容可能受版权保护，数据库可能有汇编权利，平台条款可能限制抓取与再分发，个人信息受隐私规则约束，真人和声音还涉及肖像与人格权。每一层都要有单独的核对结果，不能用一个“CC”或“公开”概括。“Publicly accessible” does not mean “may be trained on, commercialised and redistributed”. Web content may be copyrighted, databases may carry compilation rights, platform terms may restrict crawling and redistribution, personal data is constrained by privacy rules, and real people and voices raise likeness and personality rights. Each layer needs its own check; one “CC” or “public” label cannot cover them.

本页只给核对顺序，不替任何用途下法律结论。许可与用途以授权文件、合同、平台条款和适用法律为准。拿不准时，先停用该批数据，而不是先训完再补授权。This page gives a checking order, not a legal conclusion for any use. Licences and use remain with authorisation documents, contracts, platform terms and applicable law. When unsure, stop using the batch before training, not after.

3 · 来源凭证：每一批数据都要能回到“哪一版、哪一天、什么许可”3 · Provenance: every batch must trace to a version, a date and a licence

来源凭证不是“数据来自互联网”。至少要有来源 URL 或数据集标识、抓取时间、HTTP 状态、许可快照、内容哈希、采集器与参数。网页会改、许可会换、账号会删。没有快照，半年后无法证明当时拿到的是什么。Provenance is not “the data came from the internet”. It needs at least a source URL or dataset identifier, retrieval time, HTTP status, licence snapshot, content hash, collector and parameters. Pages change, licences change and accounts disappear; without a snapshot, you cannot prove what was obtained six months later.

来源凭证要和数据分开存：数据表放内容与哈希，清单放来源与许可，日志放采集过程。这样删除或替换一条数据时，不必重写整张表。审计时也能按来源、许可、时间筛出问题批次。Store provenance separately from content: the data table holds content and hashes, the manifest holds source and licence, the log holds the collection process. Removing or replacing a record then does not require rewriting the table, and audits can filter problem batches by source, licence or date.

4 · 清洗：先定保留规则，再谈过滤强度4 · Cleaning: define what to keep before how hard to filter

清洗的目标不是“把数据弄干净”，而是“让留下来的数据能回答用途”。编码、模板、导航、重复、乱码、语言混杂要先处理。个人信息、毒性、成人内容、隐私泄露要按用途和规则处理。长尾语言、少数群体表达不能被“质量分”一锅端。Cleaning is not “make the data clean”; it is “make the retained data answer the purpose”. Fix encoding, boilerplate, navigation, duplicates, garbled text and mixed languages first. Handle personal data, toxicity, adult content and privacy leaks according to purpose and rules. Long-tail languages and minority expression must not be wiped out by one quality score.

每一条过滤规则都要有抽样证据：被删了多少、删了哪些、为什么删、对目标分布有什么影响。只报“清洗后 2TB”没有意义。要能说清从 3TB 到 2TB 失去了什么。Every filter needs sampled evidence: how much was removed, which records, why and what it did to the target distribution. “Two terabytes after cleaning” means nothing without saying what was lost between three and two.

编码与格式
做什么：统一 UTF-8、换行、HTML 去标签、PDF 抽文本
证据：解析失败率、抽样原文Encoding and format
What to do: Normalise UTF-8, line endings, strip HTML, extract PDF text
Evidence: Parse-failure rate, sampled text

模板与导航
做什么：去掉页眉页脚、菜单、广告、重复声明
证据：模板检测命中率、残留抽样Boilerplate and navigation
What to do: Remove headers, menus, ads and repeated notices
Evidence: Template hit rate, residual sample

语言识别
做什么：标注主语言与混杂比例
证据：混淆矩阵、短文本抽检Language identification
What to do: Label primary language and mixed-language ratio
Evidence: Confusion matrix, short-text audit

个人信息
做什么：识别姓名、电话、邮箱、身份证、地址、生物特征
证据：召回率、误杀率、脱敏记录Personal data
What to do: Detect names, phones, emails, IDs, addresses, biometrics
Evidence: Recall, false-positive rate, redaction log

去重
做什么：精确哈希 + 近重复（MinHash / SimHash 等）
证据：重复率、被删簇样本Deduplication
What to do: Exact hashes plus near-duplicate detection
Evidence: Duplicate rate, samples of removed clusters

质量过滤
做什么：长度、符号比、重复度、困惑度、分类器分
证据：各阈值保留率、分布变化Quality filtering
What to do: Length, symbol ratio, repetition, perplexity, classifier score
Evidence: Retention by threshold, distribution shift

安全过滤
做什么：按用途处理毒性、成人、违法与泄露内容
证据：命中类型、人工复核、申诉路径Safety filtering
What to do: Handle toxicity, adult, illegal and leaked content by purpose
Evidence: Hit types, human review, appeal path

过滤强度不是越高越好。先写“必须保留什么”，再写“必须去掉什么”。Stronger filtering is not better. Write what must be kept before what must be removed.

5 · 标注：指南、试标、双标、裁决、漂移5 · Labelling: guideline, pilot, double annotation, adjudication, drift

标注质量不是“找更聪明的人”，而是“让两个人对同一条能做出同样判断”。先写指南，明确边界例、反例、必填字段和不允许的推断。再做 30–50 条试标，统计一致性与分歧。然后双标、裁决、抽检。直接上量，最后只会得到一套无法解释的标签。Annotation quality is not “find smarter people”; it is “make two people reach the same judgement on the same item”. Write the guideline with boundary cases, counterexamples, required fields and forbidden inferences. Pilot 30–50 items, measure agreement and disputes, then double-annotate, adjudicate and audit. Scaling first only produces unexplainable labels.

一致性指标要跟任务匹配。分类看 Cohen’s kappa 或 alpha，跨度看 ICC，排序看 Kendall’s tau。指标低时先改指南和样本，不要先骂标注员。还要留一份“金标集”，每次换人、换模型预标、换指南都复跑。Agreement metrics must fit the task: kappa or alpha for categories, ICC for continuous scales, Kendall’s tau for rankings. When agreement is low, revise the guideline and samples before blaming annotators. Keep a gold set and rerun it whenever annotators, model pre-labelling or guidelines change.

指南
产出：边界例、反例、字段、判定树
验收：新人读完能独立标 10 条Guideline
Output: Boundary cases, counterexamples, fields, decision tree
Acceptance: A newcomer can label ten items independently

试标
产出：30–50 条覆盖难例的样本
验收：分歧集中在哪里已写清Pilot
Output: 30–50 items covering hard cases
Acceptance: Disagreement hotspots documented

双标
产出：每条至少两人独立标
验收：一致性达到预设阈值Double annotation
Output: At least two independent labels per item
Acceptance: Agreement reaches the preset threshold

裁决
产出：第三人或规则裁决分歧
验收：裁决理由可追溯Adjudication
Output: A third person or rule resolves disputes
Acceptance: Rationale is traceable

金标集
产出：固定、隐藏、覆盖边界
验收：换人/换预标后仍能复跑Gold set
Output: Fixed, held out, boundary-heavy
Acceptance: Reruns after annotator or pre-label changes

漂移检查
产出：按时间、来源、难度分层抽检
验收：分布漂移有报警Drift check
Output: Stratified audit by time, source and difficulty
Acceptance: Distribution drift alerts

标注不是人多就行。先让两个人对同一条做出同样判断。Annotation is not a headcount problem. First make two people reach the same judgement on the same item.

6 · 切分与污染：训练集、验证集、测试集不能只按随机切6 · Splits and contamination: train, validation and test are not a random cut

随机切分会让同一实体、同一模板、同一段文本的近重复同时出现在训练和测试。正确做法是先按实体、时间、来源或用户分组，再在组内切。对去重后的近重复做跨集检查。对公开基准做 n-gram 重叠与题目级比对。否则分数上涨只证明模型见过。A random split lets the same entity, template or near-duplicate text appear in both training and test. Group by entity, time, source or user first, then split within groups; check near-duplicates across sets; compare public benchmarks at n-gram and item level. Otherwise a higher score only proves the model has seen the test.

测试集要锁：锁定文件、锁定评分器、锁定预处理。每次调参都看测试集，等于把它变成验证集。最后需要一份从未参与选择的 holdout，哪怕小一点。Lock the test set: lock the file, the scorer and the preprocessing. Looking at it after every tuning run turns it into a validation set. Keep a holdout that never participated in selection, even if it is smaller.

按实体分组
适合：同一人物、产品、文档有多个样本
要防什么：同一实体跨集泄漏Group by entity
Use when: Multiple samples per person, product or document
Guard against: Same entity across sets

按时间切
适合：新闻、对话、交易、日志
要防什么：用未来预测过去、分布漂移Split by time
Use when: News, dialogue, transactions, logs
Guard against: Future leaking into past, distribution shift

按来源切
适合：多站点、多供应商、多设备
要防什么：来源特有模板被当能力Split by source
Use when: Multiple sites, vendors or devices
Guard against: Source-specific templates mistaken for capability

按用户切
适合：推荐、个性化、对话
要防什么：同一用户行为跨集Split by user
Use when: Recommendation, personalisation, dialogue
Guard against: Same user behaviour across sets

近重复检查
适合：网页、代码、论文、歌词
要防什么：改写、翻译、模板变体Near-duplicate check
Use when: Web, code, papers, lyrics
Guard against: Paraphrase, translation, template variants

基准污染检查
适合：使用公开评测
要防什么：题目、答案、解析进入训练Benchmark contamination
Use when: Using public benchmarks
Guard against: Items, answers or rationales in training

随机切分不是切分。先按实体、时间或来源分组。A random cut is not a split. Group by entity, time or source first.

7 · 版本与复现：数据、代码、配置、环境一起锁7 · Versions and reproduction: lock data, code, config and environment together

只存“最终数据”无法复现。要存数据哈希与清单、清洗与标注代码、切分配置、随机种子、依赖版本、硬件环境、许可快照和变更记录。数据卡要写清用途、来源、字段、规模、语言、时间范围、已知偏差、禁止用途和联系方式。Storing only the “final data” does not reproduce. Keep data hashes and manifests, cleaning and labelling code, split config, random seeds, dependency versions, hardware environment, licence snapshots and a changelog. A dataset card states purpose, sources, fields, size, languages, time range, known biases, prohibited uses and contact.

版本号不要只写日期。用“数据哈希前 8 位 + 配置版本”能区分同一天的两批。每次重建产生新哈希，旧版本保留只读。数据卡与模型卡要互相引用，否则模型出问题时无法回到训练数据。Do not version by date alone. A short data hash plus config version distinguishes two batches on the same day; every rebuild produces a new hash and old versions stay read-only. Dataset cards and model cards should reference each other, or a model incident cannot be traced back to training data.

数据内容
记录方式：逐文件 SHA-256 + 总清单哈希
复现用途：确认是不是同一批Data content
Record: Per-file SHA-256 plus manifest hash
Reproduction use: Confirm the same batch

清洗 / 标注代码
记录方式：Git 提交 + 运行命令
复现用途：重跑同一处理Cleaning / labelling code
Record: Git commit plus command
Reproduction use: Rerun the same processing

切分配置
记录方式：实体/时间字段、比例、种子
复现用途：重建同一训练测试集Split config
Record: Entity/time fields, ratios, seed
Reproduction use: Rebuild the same train/test sets

依赖与环境
记录方式：锁文件、CUDA/驱动、硬件
复现用途：解释速度与数值差异Dependencies and environment
Record: Lockfile, CUDA/driver, hardware
Reproduction use: Explain speed and numeric differences

许可快照
记录方式：条款原文、日期、来源
复现用途：证明当时的使用依据Licence snapshot
Record: Terms text, date, source
Reproduction use: Prove the basis at the time

变更记录
记录方式：增删改、原因、影响范围
复现用途：审计与回滚Changelog
Record: Adds, removes, edits, reason, impact
Reproduction use: Audit and rollback

数据卡
记录方式：用途、来源、字段、偏差、禁止用途
复现用途：给使用者的边界说明Dataset card
Record: Purpose, sources, fields, biases, prohibited uses
Reproduction use: Boundary note for users

不能复现的数据版本，等于没有版本。A data version that cannot be reproduced is not a version.

8 · 隐私与删除：先最小化，再谈“删得掉”8 · Privacy and deletion: minimise first, then discuss deletion

数据里的个人信息不只是姓名和电话。位置轨迹、设备 ID、聊天上下文、人脸、声纹、病历、客服记录都可能可识别。先做数据最小化：不需要的字段不采、不存、不传。需要保留的做脱敏、分权、加密和保留期限。Personal data is not just names and phone numbers. Location traces, device IDs, chat context, faces, voiceprints, medical records and support logs can all identify someone. Minimise first: do not collect, store or transfer fields you do not need; for what remains, redact, restrict access, encrypt and set retention.

删除要能传到训练集、索引、缓存、备份和派生数据。模型权重是否“忘记”某条数据，目前没有通用保证。能做的是停止继续使用、记录数据来源、在检索层移除、在微调数据中删除，并在模型卡里写清限制。不要把“重新训练一次”当成删除证明。Deletion must propagate to training sets, indexes, caches, backups and derived data. There is no general guarantee that model weights “forget” a record; what you can do is stop further use, record provenance, remove it from retrieval, remove it from fine-tuning data and state the limit in the model card. Do not treat one retraining run as proof of deletion.

  • 按字段做最小化：用途不需要的字段不采、不存、不传给第三方。Minimise by field: do not collect, store or send fields the purpose does not need.
  • 对直接标识符与准标识符分别处理：姓名、电话、身份证、设备 ID、位置、时间组合都可能重识别。Handle direct and quasi-identifiers separately: names, phones, IDs, device IDs, location and time combinations can re-identify.
  • 访问分权：标注、训练、评测、审计看到的数据范围不同，默认最小权限。Separate access: annotation, training, evaluation and audit see different scopes; least privilege by default.
  • 保留期限与删除流程写进合同和 runbook，不靠某个人记得删。Put retention and deletion into contracts and a runbook; do not rely on someone remembering to delete.
  • 在数据卡和模型卡写清“不能保证完全遗忘”，把检索层与微调层的删除路径分开。State in the dataset and model cards that full forgetting is not guaranteed; separate retrieval-layer and fine-tuning-layer deletion paths.
先最小化，再谈删除。模型权重里的“忘记”没有通用保证。Minimise before discussing deletion; there is no general guarantee of forgetting inside model weights.

9 · 成本：按“可用样本”算，不按“下载量”算9 · Cost: price usable examples, not downloaded volume

数据成本最容易低估的是人工与返工。下载和存储很便宜，清洗、标注、质检、法律核对和返工很贵。一批数据从 3TB 洗到 2TB，真正可用可能只有几十万条。报价或排期要按“验收通过的可用样本”算，并把标注轮次、返工比例和审核时间写进去。The most underestimated data costs are labour and rework. Download and storage are cheap; cleaning, labelling, QA, legal review and rework are not. A batch cut from three terabytes to two may yield only a few hundred thousand usable records. Quote and schedule per accepted usable example, with annotation rounds, rework rate and review time written in.

成本要分“一次性”和“持续”两部分：一次性是采集、清洗、标注、评测集建设。持续是更新、去重、漂移监控、删除请求、许可复核。只算一次性，上线后会被维护成本拖住。Split cost into one-off and ongoing: one-off covers collection, cleaning, labelling and evaluation-set construction; ongoing covers refresh, deduplication, drift monitoring, deletion requests and licence review. Counting only the one-off leaves maintenance to surprise you after launch.

获取 / 授权
单位：批 / 合同
为什么会失控：来源分散、条款逐个谈
控制办法：先做来源清单与优先级Acquisition / licensing
Unit: Batch / contract
Why it runs away: Scattered sources, term-by-term negotiation
Control: Source list and priority first

存储 / 传输
单位：GB / 月
为什么会失控：原始、中间、派生全量保留
控制办法：分层存储与保留期限Storage / transfer
Unit: GB per month
Why it runs away: Keeping raw, intermediate and derived copies
Control: Tiered storage and retention

清洗
单位：小时 / 万条
为什么会失控：规则反复改、无抽样验证
控制办法：先小批试跑，再全量Cleaning
Unit: Hours per 10k records
Why it runs away: Rules change repeatedly without sampling
Control: Pilot on a small batch before scaling

标注
单位：条 / 轮
为什么会失控：指南不清、双标变多标
控制办法：试标与一致性门槛Labelling
Unit: Items per round
Why it runs away: Unclear guideline, double annotation becomes many
Control: Pilot and agreement threshold

质检
单位：抽检比例
为什么会失控：只查总数、不查分层
控制办法：按来源/难度/时间分层QA
Unit: Audit rate
Why it runs away: Totals checked without stratification
Control: Stratify by source, difficulty and time

返工
单位：轮 / 批
为什么会失控：验收标准临时改
控制办法：验收表 + 变更单Rework
Unit: Rounds per batch
Why it runs away: Acceptance criteria change late
Control: Acceptance table plus change order

法律 / 合规
单位：次 / 批
为什么会失控：等出了问题再补
控制办法：上线前按权利层核对Legal / compliance
Unit: Review per batch
Why it runs away: Only after an incident
Control: Check by rights layer before launch

按“可用样本”算成本。下载量不是产能。Cost per usable example; downloaded volume is not throughput.

10 · 失败分支：先停批，再定位，不要先训完10 · Failure branches: stop the batch, locate the cause, do not train first

数据问题一旦进入训练，修复成本会成倍上升。看到许可不清、来源断链、标签漂移、测试泄漏或个人信息混入，先把对应批次隔离，再定位是采集、清洗、标注、切分还是版本问题。不要用“先训一版看看”换取暂时的进度。Once a data problem enters training, the fix cost multiplies. When a licence is unclear, provenance is broken, labels drift, test data leaks or personal data appears, isolate the batch first, then locate whether the cause is collection, cleaning, labelling, splitting or versioning. Do not trade a temporary schedule gain for “train one version and see”.

许可说不清
先查：来源、条款快照、授权范围
常见根因：只记了网址，没存条款
处理：停批，补授权或替换Unclear licence
Check first: Source, terms snapshot, scope
Likely cause: URL saved without terms
Fix: Stop the batch; add authorisation or replace

重复样本很多
先查：精确/近重复率、来源集中度
常见根因：多站转载、模板复制
处理：按簇去重，保留代表样本Many duplicates
Check first: Exact/near-duplicate rate, source concentration
Likely cause: Syndication, template copying
Fix: Deduplicate clusters, keep representatives

标签前后不一致
先查：指南版本、标注员、时间
常见根因：指南改了没复标
处理：重跑金标，按新指南返工Label drift
Check first: Guideline version, annotator, time
Likely cause: Guideline changed without relabelling
Fix: Rerun gold set, rework under new guideline

分数异常高
先查：训练/测试重叠、近重复、基准污染
常见根因：随机切分或题目泄漏
处理：重建分组切分，去污染后复测Suspiciously high score
Check first: Train/test overlap, near-duplicates, benchmark contamination
Likely cause: Random split or leaked items
Fix: Rebuild grouped splits, decontaminate, retest

含个人信息
先查：字段、识别器召回、人工抽检
常见根因：脱敏只做了正则
处理：隔离、脱敏、复检、记录删除Personal data present
Check first: Fields, detector recall, human audit
Likely cause: Regex-only redaction
Fix: Isolate, redact, re-audit, log deletion

类别严重失衡
先查：按来源/难度的分布
常见根因：清洗把少数类过滤掉
处理：分层保留，补采样或加权Severe class imbalance
Check first: Distribution by source and difficulty
Likely cause: Cleaning removed the minority class
Fix: Stratify, resample or reweight

来源页面已失效
先查：快照、哈希、镜像
常见根因：只存了 URL
处理：用快照复核；无法复核则下架Source page gone
Check first: Snapshot, hash, mirror
Likely cause: Only the URL was stored
Fix: Check the snapshot; remove if unverifiable

数据问题先隔离批次。先训一版只会把问题写进权重。Isolate the batch first. Training one version only writes the problem into the weights.

11 · 一条最小路径：从用途句到可复现数据卡11 · A minimum path: from purpose sentence to reproducible dataset card

下面这条路径适用于一批要用于微调、评测或 RAG 的数据。每一步都留下证据，下一才能开始。This path fits a batch intended for fine-tuning, evaluation or RAG. Each step leaves evidence before the next begins.

  • 写用途句：输入、输出、判定、使用场景和不做什么。Write the purpose sentence: input, output, judgement, use case and what it will not do.
  • 列来源与权利清单：来源、条款、授权、同意、地域、期限、禁止用途。List sources and rights: source, terms, authorisation, consent, territory, duration and prohibited uses.
  • 建来源凭证：URL/ID、抓取时间、状态、许可快照、哈希、采集参数。Build provenance: URL/ID, retrieval time, status, licence snapshot, hash and collection parameters.
  • 小批试洗：编码、模板、语言、PII、去重、质量与安全过滤，记录保留率。Pilot cleaning: encoding, boilerplate, language, PII, deduplication, quality and safety filtering; record retention.
  • 写标注指南并试标 30–50 条：边界例、反例、一致性、分歧。Write the labelling guideline and pilot 30–50 items: boundary cases, counterexamples, agreement, disputes.
  • 正式标注与裁决：双标、金标、抽检、漂移记录。Annotate and adjudicate: double annotation, gold set, audit, drift log.
  • 分组切分与污染检查：实体/时间/来源分组，近重复与基准比对。Grouped splits and contamination checks: entity/time/source groups, near-duplicate and benchmark comparison.
  • 锁版本：数据哈希、代码、配置、种子、环境、许可快照、变更记录。Lock versions: data hash, code, config, seed, environment, licence snapshot, changelog.
  • 写数据卡并交付：用途、来源、字段、规模、偏差、禁止用途、删除路径。Write the dataset card and deliver: purpose, sources, fields, size, biases, prohibited uses and deletion path.
  • 上线后监控：重复率、分布漂移、来源失效、删除请求、许可变化。Monitor after launch: duplicate rate, distribution drift, source failure, deletion requests, licence changes.
最小路径不是“最快路径”。它是能复现、能审计、能删除的最短闭环。The minimum path is not the fastest path; it is the shortest reproducible, auditable and deletable loop.
