AI 视频：先写清验收标准，再选模型AI video: write the acceptance criteria before choosing a model

视频不是一张会动的图。交付物包含时长、画幅、叙事、镜头连续性、声音、字幕、版权与可重做性。先写脚本和分镜，再决定用文生视频、图生视频还是编辑；否则只会在「看起来还行」的片段里反复抽卡，最后拼不成一条能发的片子。Video is not a still image that moves. The deliverable includes duration, aspect ratio, narrative, shot continuity, sound, captions, rights and repeatability. Write the script and shot list before choosing text-to-video, image-to-video or editing. Otherwise you keep rolling for clips that “look okay” and still cannot assemble a releasable film.

本页是 Specul 的 AI 视频制作判断框架，不是模型榜单。模型能力、价格与输出许可以所链页面和原始来源为准。This is Specul’s decision framework for AI video production, not a model leaderboard. Capabilities, prices and output licences remain with the linked pages and original sources.

先定片子的交付标准与镜头表，再选生成方式；单段 demo 不等于成片。Fix the delivery spec and shot list first, then choose the generation method. A single demo clip is not a finished film.

1 · 先写交付标准：一条片子要被谁、在哪里、怎么看1 · Write the delivery spec: who watches it, where and how

在写提示词之前，先把交付标准写在一张纸上：时长、画幅、分辨率、平台、是否需要字幕、是否有音乐、截止时间、谁审片。标准不同，工具链就不同。竖屏 15 秒和横屏 3 分钟不是同一个问题。Before writing a prompt, put the delivery spec on one page: duration, aspect ratio, resolution, platform, captions, music, deadline and who approves. Different specs need different pipelines. A 15-second vertical clip and a three-minute horizontal film are not the same problem.

还要写清「不能接受什么」：人脸不像、口型不对、文字乱码、物理错误、品牌元素缺失、音乐版权不明。把不可接受项提前列出，审片时才有共同语言，而不是每个人凭感觉说「怪怪的」。Also write what is unacceptable: wrong likeness, bad lip sync, garbled text, broken physics, missing brand elements, unclear music rights. Listing unacceptable outcomes in advance gives reviewers a shared language instead of everyone saying “it feels off”.

2 · 脚本与分镜：把「我想讲的」拆成可生成的镜头2 · Script and shot list: turn the idea into generatable shots

先写一句话故事，再拆镜头。每个镜头只承担一个叙事任务：交代地点、展示动作、给出反应、完成转折。一个镜头里塞三个动作，生成模型很难保持连贯，剪辑时也没有可用的替代镜头。Write a one-sentence story, then break it into shots. Each shot carries one narrative job: establish a place, show an action, give a reaction, deliver a turn. A shot with three actions is hard for a generation model to keep coherent and leaves no alternate takes for editing.

分镜表至少要有：镜号、画面、动作、镜头运动、时长、声音、参考图。参考图不是「风格板」越多越好；每个角色、场景和关键道具，最好有一张稳定的主参考，再准备少量角度参考。参考越乱，角色越容易漂移。A shot list needs at least: shot number, image, action, camera movement, duration, sound and reference. More style boards is not better. Each character, location and key prop should have one stable master reference plus a few angle references. Messier references make characters drift more.

先做关键帧，再做视频。关键帧是成本最低的确认点：构图、角色、服装、光线、文字位置都能在这一步改。直接文生视频，等于把构图、表演、运镜和时序一次性押注，失败后很难知道该改哪一项。Make keyframes before video. Keyframes are the cheapest confirmation point: composition, character, wardrobe, lighting and text placement can all be fixed there. Going straight to text-to-video bets composition, performance, camera and timing all at once; when it fails, you cannot tell what to change.

判断句：如果分镜表上写不出「这个镜头要让观众知道什么」，这个镜头大概率可以删。Rule of thumb: if the shot list cannot say what the audience should learn from a shot, that shot can probably be cut.

3 · 生成方式：文生、图生、编辑各管一段3 · Generation modes: text, image and editing each own a stage

文生视频适合探索概念和气氛，控制力弱；图生视频适合从已确认的关键帧出发，保持构图和角色；视频编辑适合改已有素材的局部，而不是从零生成。把三者串起来，比找一个「什么都能做」的模型更现实。Text-to-video is good for exploring concepts and mood but offers weak control. Image-to-video starts from an approved keyframe and preserves composition and character. Video editing changes parts of existing footage rather than generating from scratch. Chaining the three is more realistic than finding one model that does everything.

选模型时不要只看演示片。要核对：支持多长镜头、什么画幅、是否带音频、是否支持首尾帧、是否支持参考图、输出分辨率、商用条款、是否有水印。演示片通常只展示最好的一条，不展示失败率和重试成本。Do not choose a model from its demo reel alone. Check supported shot length, aspect ratios, audio, first/last frame, reference images, output resolution, commercial terms and watermarking. Demos show the best take, not the failure rate or retry cost.

把「生成」当成采样，不是一次成片。同一个镜头生成多条，按构图、动作、稳定性、可用时长筛选，再决定补拍或换模型。接受「多条里选一条」的流程，比追求「一条就完美」更可控。Treat generation as sampling, not one-shot filmmaking. Generate several takes per shot, select by composition, action, stability and usable duration, then decide whether to regenerate or switch models. Accepting “choose one from many” is more controllable than chasing a perfect first take.

4 · 声音：观众对「不像」的容忍度，比对画面低4 · Sound: audiences tolerate “wrong” picture less than wrong sound

一条片子的可信度，很大一部分来自声音。人声、环境声、音乐和音效要分开处理：人声要能听清，音乐不能压过解说，音效要和动作对齐。先把声音做成时间轴，再谈混音。A film’s credibility comes largely from sound. Treat voice, ambience, music and effects separately: voice must be intelligible, music must not overpower narration, effects must align with action. Build sound on the timeline before mixing.

AI 配音要核对发音、断句、情绪和口型。短句比长句稳，先分段生成再拼；多语言版本要重新审听，不要假设字幕翻译等于配音。音乐和音效同样要核对授权范围：商用、改编、平台分发是不是都覆盖。For AI voice, check pronunciation, phrasing, emotion and lip sync. Short lines are more stable; generate in segments and assemble. For multilingual versions, re-listen rather than assuming subtitles equal dubbing. Music and SFX also need licence checks: commercial use, adaptation and platform distribution.

如果片子没有对白，声音也不能省。环境底噪、动作音效和音乐决定了画面「有没有重量」。完全静音的 AI 视频，很容易显得像幻灯片。If the film has no dialogue, sound still cannot be skipped. Ambience, action sounds and music give images weight. A completely silent AI video easily feels like a slideshow.

5 · 剪辑与一致性：生成镜头，不生成整片5 · Editing and consistency: generate shots, not the whole film

把整片拆成镜头，是为了让失败局部化。一个镜头坏了，只重做那一段；整片一次生成，失败就得全部重来。剪辑阶段还要处理节奏、转场、色彩和字幕，这些通常比多生成一条更重要。Breaking the film into shots localises failure. If one shot breaks, redo only that segment; generate the whole film at once and a failure forces a full restart. Editing also handles rhythm, transitions, colour and captions, often more important than one more generation.

一致性靠三件事：稳定参考、稳定构图、稳定描述。角色服装、发型、场景光线、镜头焦段都要写进镜头表；不要在每个提示词里换同义词。生成模型对描述变化很敏感，统一词汇能减少漂移。Consistency comes from three things: stable references, stable composition and stable descriptions. Wardrobe, hair, lighting and focal length belong in the shot list; do not swap synonyms in every prompt. Generation models are sensitive to wording changes; a consistent vocabulary reduces drift.

输出规格要一次定好：分辨率、帧率、色彩空间、字幕安全区。后期再改画幅会重新构图，可能把关键动作裁掉。先按最终平台做构图，再生成。Fix output specs early: resolution, frame rate, colour space and caption-safe area. Changing aspect ratio in post recomposes the image and may crop key action. Compose for the final platform before generating.

6 · 成本：按「成片秒」算，不按「生成次数」算6 · Cost: count approved seconds, not generation calls

视频成本最容易算错的地方，是把「生成一次」当「成片一秒」。真实成本包括：每条镜头的多次生成、失败重试、放大、存储、配音、音乐、剪辑和审片时间。分母应该是审片通过的成片秒数，不是生成总时长。The easiest video cost mistake is treating one generation as one finished second. Real cost includes multiple takes per shot, failed retries, upscaling, storage, voice, music, editing and review time. The denominator should be approved finished seconds, not generated seconds.

一个可用的算式：总成本 = 生成次数 × 单次成本 + 放大与存储 + 声音与音乐 + 剪辑人力 + 审片与返工。再除以成片秒数，得到「每秒成片成本」。这个数字才能和外包、实拍或其他工具比较。A usable formula: total cost = generation calls × cost per call + upscaling and storage + sound and music + editing labour + review and rework. Divide by approved finished seconds to get cost per finished second. Only then can you compare with outsourcing, live action or another tool.

先做 15 秒样片，再决定要不要做 3 分钟。样片能暴露角色漂移、口型、文字、音乐和审片分歧；直接做长片，返工成本会成倍放大。Make a 15-second sample before committing to three minutes. The sample exposes character drift, lip sync, text, music and review disagreements; going straight to a long film multiplies rework.

7 · 版权与平台：画面、声音、字体、肖像要分开清7 · Rights and platforms: clear image, sound, fonts and likeness separately

AI 生成不等于没有版权问题。要分开核对：输入素材是否允许用于生成；模型输出条款是否允许商用；音乐、配音、字体、品牌元素是否有独立授权；是否使用真实人物肖像或声音；平台是否要求标注 AI 生成。AI generation does not mean no rights issues. Check separately: whether input assets may be used for generation; whether the model’s output terms allow commercial use; whether music, voice, fonts and brand elements have independent licences; whether real likeness or voice is used; whether the platform requires AI disclosure.

「模型说可以商用」不等于「这条片子可以商用」。模型条款管的是输出，不管输入素材、音乐、肖像和平台规则。四类权利要分别留证：来源、许可、范围、期限。“The model says commercial use is allowed” does not mean “this film is cleared”. Model terms cover outputs, not input assets, music, likeness or platform rules. Keep evidence for four things separately: source, licence, scope and term.

发布前做一次清单：输入素材来源、输出条款、音乐许可、字体许可、肖像/声音授权、平台披露、水印。任何一项写「不知道」，就先别发。Before publishing, run a checklist: input asset sources, output terms, music licence, font licence, likeness/voice permission, platform disclosure and watermark. If any item says “unknown”, do not publish yet.

判断句：如果一条片子只能证明「画面是 AI 生成的」，不能证明「声音、字体、肖像和输入素材都清过」，它还不算可商用交付。Rule of thumb: if a film can prove only that the picture was AI-generated, not that sound, fonts, likeness and input assets are cleared, it is not a commercial deliverable.

8 · 失败分支：按镜头修，不要整片重抽8 · Failure branches: fix the shot, not the whole film

视频失败通常可以定位到镜头级：角色漂移、动作断裂、文字乱码、口型不对、物理错误、风格不统一。先判断是参考问题、提示词问题、模型能力问题还是后期问题，再决定重做哪一段。Video failures usually localise to a shot: character drift, broken action, garbled text, bad lip sync, physics errors, style mismatch. Decide whether it is a reference, prompt, model-capability or post issue, then redo that segment.

9 · 最小落地路径9 · A minimum path to production

先做 15 秒、3 个镜头、带声音的样片。样片通过审片，再扩到 60 秒；不要一上来做长片。Make a 15-second, three-shot sample with sound. If it passes review, extend to 60 seconds; do not start with a long film.

  • 写交付标准与不可接受项。Write the delivery spec and unacceptable outcomes.
  • 写一句话故事与分镜表，每个镜头只做一件事。Write a one-sentence story and shot list; one job per shot.
  • 做关键帧，先确认构图、角色、服装、光线。Make keyframes; approve composition, character, wardrobe and lighting.
  • 选生成方式：文生探索，图生锁定，编辑修局部。Choose the mode: text to explore, image to lock, editing to fix locally.
  • 每个镜头生成多条，按稳定性与可用时长筛选。Generate multiple takes per shot; select by stability and usable duration.
  • 先做声音时间轴，再做混音与字幕。Build the sound timeline before mixing and captions.
  • 按成片秒算成本，保留重试与返工预算。Cost by finished seconds; budget retries and rework.
  • 清四类权利，做平台披露，留回滚素材。Clear four rights classes, add platform disclosure, keep source assets for revisions.
四个常见误区：只写提示词不写分镜；一条生成当整片；忽略声音与授权；把模型演示片当交付标准。Four common mistakes: prompts without a shot list; one generation as the whole film; ignoring sound and rights; treating a model demo as the delivery standard.
